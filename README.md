## Quokka

Quokka is a hobby UCI chess engine that I developed in C++ from scratch during the winter of 2014.

It plays at an [Elo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elo_rating_system) rating of approximately 1800.

## Play it live right now!

You can play against Quokka right now in your browser!
Check it out [here](https://mattbruv.github.io/Quokka/)!

In the past, Quokka used to have a bot account on lichess.org:
https://lichess.org/@/QuokkaBot
but I no longer host it.
